NEZZEL BROWSER AD AND TRACKER LISTS

The Nezzel browser blocks ads and trackers with network rules taken from these
lists (page-hiding rules removed). Snapshot: src-tauri/src/catch/filters/network.txt,
made by scripts/fetch-filters.mjs; its header names each list's version.
